About the Doctor / Surgeon

A Surgeon Built on Excellence & Experience

Dr. Md. Abu Bakar Siddiq Faysal completed his SSC from Cumilla Zila School and his HSC from Notre Dame College, Dhaka. He completed his MBBS (Batch 2005) from Sir Salimullah Medical College, Dhaka — one of Bangladesh's oldest and most prestigious medical institutions. He subsequently completed his FCPS in Surgery, the highest surgical qualification in the country.

He is currently serving patients at Cumilla Medical College Hospital — Burn, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Department, as a General, Laparoscopic, Colorectal, Breast, Burn and Plastic Surgery Specialist. He is also serving patients at Gomoti Hospital (Pvt.) Ltd. — one of the most renowned private hospitals in Cumilla. His expertise in minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery provides faster recovery and less pain for his patients.

Patient Education

Cancer Awareness & Early Detection

Early detection dramatically improves survival rates. Dr. Faysal encourages every patient to know the warning signs.

⚠️ Warning Signs to Watch For

  • A new lump or mass felt anywhere in the body
  • A wound or sore that does not heal normally
  • Unusual bleeding or abnormal discharge
  • Changes in bowel habits or blood in stool
  • Persistent cough or hoarseness of voice
  • Difficulty swallowing or prolonged indigestion
  • Change in size, colour, or shape of a mole
  • Unexplained, significant weight loss

✅ Prevention & Early Action

  • Completely avoid tobacco and smoking
  • Maintain a balanced diet and regular physical activity
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure — protect your skin
  • Get regular health check-ups and cancer screenings
  • Practise regular self-examination of breast and body
  • Do not delay — see a specialist at the first sign of symptoms
⚕️ Medical Disclaimer: The above information is for general awareness only and is not a substitute for a specific diagnosis or treatment. If you notice any of the symptoms above, please visit the chamber directly for expert evaluation without delay.
